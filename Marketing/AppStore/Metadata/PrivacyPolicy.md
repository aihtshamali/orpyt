# Orpyt Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 18, 2026

Orpyt is designed to keep time zone, weather, and meeting context useful without collecting personal data for the developer.

## Data Collection

Orpyt does not collect, sell, or share personal data with the developer.

Orpyt does not use developer-operated analytics, advertising tracking, or a user account system.

## Calendar Access

If you enable calendar features, Orpyt requests read-only calendar access through macOS.

Calendar access is used to show upcoming meeting context, Today’s Plan, and available meeting join links inside the app. Orpyt does not create, edit, delete, upload, or sell calendar events.

Calendar data is processed locally on your Mac.

## Weather

If you enable weather features, Orpyt uses Apple WeatherKit to load weather for the clock locations you configure.

Weather locations are based on selected cities or time zones. Orpyt does not use precise device location unless that behavior is added in a future version and clearly requested from you first.

## Subscriptions

Orpyt Pro purchases and subscription status are handled by Apple through StoreKit. Orpyt receives subscription entitlement status from Apple so it can unlock Pro features.

The developer does not receive your payment card details.

## Settings

Your Orpyt preferences, including selected clocks, labels, display settings, and feature preferences, are stored locally using macOS app storage.

## External Links

Orpyt may open GitHub Discussions when you choose to suggest a feature. GitHub is a third-party service with its own privacy policy.
